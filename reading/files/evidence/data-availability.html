Data and code availability

The package includes original explanatory text, source-equivalent offline arithmetic, a frame verifier, deliberately artificial fixtures, aggregate run data and real relative delivery bins.

Complete historic ambient RF recordings, decoded traffic, private machine/network records, source conversations and operational archives remain private. Their unmodified identities are retained locally. No promise of future access or third-party disclosure is made.

Public readers can repeat synthetic protocol/error tests, verify any compatible recording they supply, recompute cadence/count/byte arithmetic and reproduce the available delivery/filter figures. They cannot repeat the complete historical payload/wire/health audits from this reduced public export. A claim based on such an audit is labelled accordingly.

Manufacturer binaries and complete patched images are excluded. Source-only device recipes are included under the owner's selected GPL-2.0-or-later grant for original code, with upstream terms preserved; original report/documentation uses CC BY 4.0. The recipes reproduce tested builds without installing a receiver; the operational acquisition/recovery controller remains private. The project explanation distinguishes experimental sample streaming from a practical general-purpose receiver.

Every released derivative receives a separate hash and transformation description. Artificial fixtures are identified in filenames, metadata and captions. Relative receipt bins remove absolute host timestamps and retain no waveform samples.

The 0.3.0 selection includes the separate STEP0 known-source demonstration:244,160 frames/250,019,840 words/300.025188989 host seconds, two bounded source bursts and 80/80 preset RF chunks. The sanitized predeclared plan, all 80 ON chunk fits and matched OFF summaries, frozen mathematical method and standalone decision reproducer permit recalculation of the acceptance decisions, frequency stability and minimum ON/OFF95 ratio. OFF95 percentiles remain supplied bound fit summaries: individual OFF fit rows are not available in the selected saved response, so their percentiles cannot be independently recomputed here. Original waveforms are withheld; this is fitted-value decision replication, not waveform refitting, a source-timing audit or independent hardware replication. Forty optional neighbor medians remain unavailable (null), with explicit availability/exclusion counts; conditional floors are descriptive. Exact native and full module rebuilds pass. The private controller is withheld and the hardware experiment is not turnkey. See known-signal findings, derivative provenance and source build.
